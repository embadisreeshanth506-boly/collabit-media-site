A website with a clear job to do

Before choosing a layout, identify what a visitor should understand and do. A service business may need enquiries, while a product brand may need visitors to explore a catalogue. A useful website connects that goal to a simple navigation structure, clear service descriptions, and a visible next step.

Our website development conversations begin with your audience, your offer, and the content you already have. That provides a practical foundation for a site that reflects your business instead of a generic collection of pages.

What to include in your website brief

Describe your core services, the customers you want to reach, and the questions they ask before buying. Include your brand assets, existing domain, preferred contact method, and any integrations that need to be considered.

A responsive website should make the important content usable on a phone as well as a desktop. Search-friendly page titles, descriptive links, accessible headings, and a sensible page structure belong in the plan from the beginning.

Landing page or a full business website?

A landing page suits one focused offer or campaign. A multi-page business website gives separate services room for their own explanations, examples, and frequently asked questions. Choose the structure that matches the decisions your visitors need to make.

If your offer changes regularly, discuss how content will be updated and who will own those updates. Hosting, domain access, ongoing maintenance, and analytics should be agreed as part of the project scope.

From design direction to launch

A useful project sequence is to agree the brief, outline the content, review the visual direction, build the pages, and check the important journeys before launch. Timelines and costs depend on the content, functionality, and review process; they should be scoped to your project.

Questions before you begin

Can I discuss a project from another country?

Yes. Collabit Media welcomes website project enquiries from worldwide clients. Include your location and preferred working hours in the brief so communication can be planned.

Does a new website automatically rank in Google?

No. A crawlable, well-structured website provides a foundation. Search visibility also depends on relevance, competition, useful content, and signals built over time.

What should I prepare before requesting a website?

Bring your business goals, a list of pages or services, brand assets, examples of designs you like, and a budget range. Identify any booking, payment, or account features separately.
