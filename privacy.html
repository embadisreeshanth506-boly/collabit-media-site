Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Collabit Media collects, uses, and protects your information when you visit our website or contact us through our services.

Information We Collect

  • Contact details you provide, such as name, email, phone number, and WhatsApp number.
  • Form responses and project details submitted through our contact and consultation forms.
  • Usage data from analytics tools, including page visits, clicks, and device information.

How We Use Your Data

  • To respond to inquiries, send proposals, and manage client communication.
  • To improve our website, services, and marketing performance.
  • To measure conversions from ads, including call clicks and form submissions.

Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies from Google Analytics and Google Ads to understand website traffic, optimize campaigns, and capture conversions. These tools may collect anonymous usage data and help us identify how visitors interact with our site.

Phone and WhatsApp Contact

When you click our phone or WhatsApp buttons, we may record the click event for analytics and advertising tracking. This helps us measure the effectiveness of our website and advertising campaigns.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. However, no system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute protection.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at hello@collabitmedia.com or use the contact form on our website.
