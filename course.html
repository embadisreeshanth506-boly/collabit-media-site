Hands-on project

Plan your first AI pilot

Pick one task from the course and write a one-page plan you could share with your team.

  1. Describe the task, who owns it, and how long it takes today.
  2. List the sources AI can use and what a useful draft looks like.
  3. Name the reviewer and the checks they’ll make before approving the result.
  4. Choose a two-week test and a measure such as time to approval or corrections per draft.
