03 / A little more about myBrain

Good questions.

What is myBrain?

myBrain is a second-brain and personal-assistant app that remembers information you provide, including contacts, notes, reminders, and personal details.

Does myBrain already know my information?

The app’s described experience is based on the information you feed it. This page does not claim automatic access to your contacts, messages, or other apps.

Where can I download it?

An official download link has not yet been added to this website.

Where can I find privacy and storage information?

myBrain’s privacy policy and details about storage, permissions, and deletion have not yet been published here. These will need to be provided separately; Fitmaker’s privacy notice does not cover myBrain.
