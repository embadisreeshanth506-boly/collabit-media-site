Neon Rush Privacy Policy

Draft updated: 25 October 2026 · Operator: Collabit Media

Draft — not ready for Google Play submission.This notice is incomplete. The advertising service, remaining data practices, retention, security, and children’s settings must be confirmed before this becomes the final policy.

About the game

Neon Rush is an obstacle-running game operated by Collabit Media. The developer describes the intended audience as ages 8 and above.

Usage information and Google Analytics

Neon Rush uses Google Analytics to measure app usage, including time spent in the game. The developer reports that the game does not request personal details from players.

Google Analytics for apps uses an app-instance identifier to measure interactions. Usage measurement can also involve device information. Not requesting personal details does not mean that no data is processed by these services. The game’s exact SDK configuration and additional automatically collected events still need verification.

Read Google’s explanation of Analytics data handling and the Google Privacy Policy.

Advertising

Neon Rush displays Google advertising. The specific advertising SDK, data it processes, and its settings for children and ad personalization are awaiting confirmation.

Accounts and permissions

Neon Rush has no login system and does not require a player account. Cloud saves, requested permissions, and on-device storage have not yet been confirmed.

Retention, deletion, and security

The final policy will describe actual retention periods, deletion options, and security measures for the game and its providers. For privacy enquiries, email collabitmedia@gmail.com. A game-specific deletion process has not yet been confirmed.

Children

The stated audience includes children. Child-directed advertising and analytics settings must be verified and described before the final policy is used for the Play listing.
