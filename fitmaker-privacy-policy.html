Fitmaker · Privacy information

Privacy policy.
Draft for review.

This notice is incomplete. It records confirmed information about Fitmaker while additional data-handling details are being clarified. It is not a final privacy policy.

Who operates Fitmaker?

Fitmaker is operated by Collabit Media. For privacy questions, contact collabitmedia@gmail.com.

This notice concerns the Fitmaker Android app, package com.thetechtree.fitmaker, which includes calorie intake and burn tracking, workout tracking, AI workout recommendations, and posture correction.

Google sign-in

Fitmaker requires users to sign in using Google. The specific account information received by Fitmaker, where it is stored, and how long it is retained are pending confirmation.

Fitness information and AI features

The app supports calorie and workout tracking, AI workout recommendations, and posture correction. Storage locations, data sent to AI providers, provider identities, and retention practices have not yet been documented in this notice.

Video and footage

The operator states that no video or footage is stored. This statement does not establish whether footage is processed only on-device or transmitted for processing without storage; that detail is still pending confirmation.

Advertising

Fitmaker uses Google AdMob for advertising. The app’s advertising settings, data collection through the advertising SDK, consent choices, and any child-directed configuration remain pending confirmation.

Deleting your data

The operator confirms that users can delete their data. Exact deletion steps, the scope of deletion, completion times, and any retention exceptions have not yet been documented here.

For questions about deletion, contact collabitmedia@gmail.com. A specific deletion workflow or processing deadline is not promised by this draft.

Age and children

The operator has supplied an age of “3+”. Whether this describes the store content rating or the age allowed to create an account remains unconfirmed. Children’s account eligibility, parental consent, and advertising safeguards require clarification before this policy can be finalised.

What remains to be completed

The final policy needs to explain the information accessed and collected, its purposes, storage and processing locations, service providers and sharing, security practices, retention and deletion, user choices, and children’s privacy practices. This draft should not be treated as confirmation that these requirements have been satisfied.
